WHEN YOU ARE BACK AT THE MAC

The first Gmail review

Use one QR in one fresh draft. You do not need to download/import recipe files or test every symbol yet. The Mac runs Chrome; the Samsung phone scans the Mac screen.

First, update your existing extension to 0.4.2
  1. Download the extension ZIP and extract it.
  2. Replace the files inside the same unpacked extension folder you already use. Keep that folder in place.
  3. Open chrome://extensions, click Aqrobat’s Reload, and confirm version 0.4.2.

Keeping the same folder/extension ID preserves the local library. If you reinstall instead, export Recipe backups first. This task did not change your installed copy.

  1. Open Aqrobat’s toolbar → Open QR workbench.
  2. Enter https://example.com. Choose ⚫️ / Black circle, 1 across × 1 row, Menlo, and 328 px. Leave ECC M, Thicken glyph ink, and Boost ECC if it fits enabled. Click Generate QR.
  3. Under the preview, enter the name Email test → Save for insertion.
  4. Switch to Gmail → Compose a fresh draft with no recipient. Type Before the QR, then press Enter to leave a blank line. In the compose window’s More options menu, ensure Plain text mode is off. Google’s instructions.
  5. On the Gmail tab, open Aqrobat’s toolbar. Select Email test → Choose where to insert.
  6. Click the blank line in the message body. In the helper panel, keep Formatted text · email / rich editors and 328 px. Click Preview → Insert QR.
  7. Inspect and scan the actual Gmail draft with Samsung Camera. Does it recognize https://example.com? Scanning the helper preview is a separate test.
  8. With the cursor in the message body, press ⌘Z. Did the QR disappear while Before the QR stayed?
That is enough for the first pass. Report insertion, draft scan, and Undo. You can leave the draft unsent. Saved drafts and received email can follow later as separate tests.
If you get stuck
  • No saved QR: use the extension’s workbench, then Save for insertion. Creating it only on the website does not add it to the extension.
  • Too narrow: widen Gmail’s compose window first. If you change the insertion width, report that number.
  • Refusal or changed layout: record the panel’s exact message and the step where it happened. Use Undo if necessary. A screenshot is optional; hide private email details.
  • No scan: that is useful feedback. Keep it separate from whether the layout looks right. Do not manually add spaces for the first recorded case.

PLANNING ONLY

A QR hidden in readable prose

I understand the target: the same visible block should read as words to a person and as a QR to a normal scanner. First prove a block of real words, then grammar, then a coherent paragraph. We would judge meaning, scanning, and how hidden it looks separately.

My hypothesis is that word choice and local ink placement can help. Letters’ average darkness is only part of it: spaces, line breaks, font metrics, and the locations of QR patterns also matter. Standard QR guidance calls for a clear four-module border. DENSO WAVE guidance.

Track A · Strict text

Real words and ordinary punctuation; one standard font, size, weight, and color. Choose words and line layout. Record awkward gaps or forced language.

Track B · Typography-assisted

Still real selectable words, with recorded weight, size, or spacing adjustments. Results depend on those styles. Report them separately from plain text.

Neither track gets a hidden solid QR underneath, a picture posing as text, or conventional QR squares substituted for its corners. Custom decoding of hidden data would be an adjacent project, not success at ordinary QR scanning.

The first pilot I recommend

  1. Calibrate existing jsQR and a pinned independent ZXing decoder with clean controls. Measure two existing fonts using a small original word list.
  2. Try real-word candidates, starting with a short payload. Keep all results and test unchanged visible rasters at three scales.
  3. Use blur/threshold/crop variants only as separately labeled diagnostics.
  4. Hand back a small HTML board, original text/styles, screenshots, and exact-payload receipts. Phone and print observations stay separate.

First checkpoint: at most 128 candidates per track, 60 minutes of execution, or 50 MB of generated evidence. Stop at the first limit and report what happened. These bounds keep the first pass manageable; they do not decide whether longer research is worthwhile.

Coherent-paragraph work comes after the structural pilot. No new chat, agent swarm, GPU work, or Astra switch is needed for the first pass. I recommend keeping this chat and current model/effort.

Related work I actually opened
  • Optimized Text Embedding in QR Codes (2016 thesis) combines word art/text with QR images using layout analysis and character deformation. Its abstract does not establish a natural paragraph made only of unchanged standard-font letters.
  • FontCode hides data using glyph perturbations and its own decoding system. It is related, but does not establish ordinary-camera QR recovery from prose.
  • Text2QR (2024) uses text prompts to generate aesthetic QR imagery. The visible output is not the ordinary-word paragraph you described.

This was a bounded project-description/abstract review, not an exhaustive novelty search or a replication. I have not generated or tested a prose candidate.
