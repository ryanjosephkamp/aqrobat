## Objective and acceptance

Retain real readable selectable story text, natural character shapes, and visible
foreground styling as in band-023. No hidden solid QR modules, glyph backgrounds,
images standing in for text, invisible duplicates, runtime AI or remote generator.
The exact initial payload is AQROBAT-TEST; a qualifying construction must also
be tested with the exact URL https://example.com/ including its final slash.

Primary screening: unchanged native browser PNGs, device scale 1, no browser zoom,
external blur, resampling, thresholding, cropping or supplied finder geometry.
ReadBarcodes(image) and jsQR(rgba,width,height) omit options; OpenCV's ordinary
QRCodeDetector().detectAndDecode receives the same PNG. Expected strings are
compared after decoding. No reader-option search. A promoted software candidate
must recover its exact payload in at least two independent engines, retain
readable visible words on visual inspection, and preserve exact source selection.
Phone, physical-print, clipboard transfer and arbitrary-application paste remain
separate owner/platform acceptance, never inferred from software or a viewport.

Old strict native-letter finder metrics are diagnostic research filters, not a
necessary acceptance gate for this styled-story lane. Any optional ordinary
Vision/ZBar/ArUco checks will be separately preregistered and counted; options
of one engine are not counted as independent engines.

## Bounds and method

Maximum 64 new candidate captures, 8 resource captures and 12 conventional
controls across all batches, plus exact immediate-repeat attempts. Incremental
evidence cap 128 MB, with 12 MB reserved for verification and handback. Each
batch freezes candidate specs, source hashes, expected payloads, reader options,
and all slots before its first capture. Failed setups and unused slots remain.
No unchanged failed candidate is retried selectively. A source change starts a
new explicitly documented batch. Any wrong payload, repeat mismatch, network
request, source-selection discrepancy, or preservation failure stops its batch.
Reader failures/timeouts are retained and distinguished from negative results.

First replay the saved band-023 and phase-14 PNGs, plus a conventional control.
Then test a small fixed batch addressing density and the relationship between
module pitch and default readers' sampling scales, while keeping letters native
and legible. Use current measurements and past failed batches to select a
specific hypothesis; do not silently repeat the earlier gray/font sweeps.
