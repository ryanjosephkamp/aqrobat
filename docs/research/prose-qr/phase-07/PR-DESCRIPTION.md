## Summary

Copying QR characters into editors can lose their layout. Codex built Aqrobat
with deterministic text/emoji generation, image and styled-text exports, a static
demo, private package/CLI, and an unpacked Make → Save → Insert extension.
Product 0.4.2 and all three original downloads remain unchanged by this research.

Readable prose QR recovery remains unsolved. Earlier styled-prose and uniform-black
ASCII/TXT configured-reader proofs are separate preserved results. Ryan's earlier
full PNGs fail both Samsung scanners; no new phone acceptance is claimed.
Phase 04 preserves 109 failed text/display views and its replay limitation;
phase 05 preserves 40 failed native captures and a perfect-center counterexample.

Phase 06 isolates finder geometry: 39 native finder-only layouts return incorrect
ordinary location. Phase 07 adds two native font sizes and four fixed source-row
guards: five native captures also fail intended location, and one exact source
is rejected for width before capture. These incomplete layouts contain no
payload and are not failed link scans or phone candidates.

A passive trace identifies one concrete mechanism: a 96-pixel middle band joins
an older 4-pixel stroke region, then ends 5 pixels wide. Its scored size is 6.33
pixels; the solid control remains 72 throughout. Larger native letters and the
fixed row guards do not solve it. The [latest checkpoint page](https://ryanjosephkamp.github.io/aqrobat/docs/research/prose-qr/phase-07/)
explains the failure using retained measured geometry and provides a safe
continuation prompt. Research remains incomplete.

## Known issues

No accepted readable, inconspicuous, meaningful prose with ordinary-reader and
normal-phone recovery. Heavy joined letters, synthetic T/E/I words and stripe-like
I rows are diagnostic relaxations. Center-axis agreement and native font size
are insufficient in the tested cases. The observed join mechanism is local;
it does not prove a universal cause or impossibility.

No new phone, print, Gmail, native clipboard or owner installed-extension
observations. Earlier phone-case count and viewing conditions remain unknown.
Phase-04 small-image replay still matches only six of eight earlier hashes.
Emoji atlas and dedicated project migration remain deferred. The next renderer
objective needs stable two-dimensional native finder spans and legibility before
another full-QR sweep, then exact independent recovery and phone acceptance.
