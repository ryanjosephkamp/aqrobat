## Summary

Copying QR characters into editors can lose their layout. Codex built Aqrobat
with deterministic text/emoji generation, image and styled-text exports, a static
demo, private package/CLI, and an unpacked Make → Save → Insert extension.
Product 0.4.2 and its original downloads remain unchanged by the prose research.

Readable prose QR scanning remains unsolved. Ryan's prior full PNGs fail both
Samsung scanners. Earlier styled-prose and uniform-black ASCII/TXT results are
preserved configured-reader proofs, separate from ordinary-reader/phone success.
Phase 04 retains 109 failed text/display views and its known replay limitation.

Phase 05 adds 40 native text captures. All fail jsQR, default ZXing and the
retained baseline. Source-aligned center correctness can reach 625/625 while
finder axes remain fragmented into dozens of short runs. Full-paragraph feedback
improves the model objective, but all four selected native outputs still fail.
The [new checkpoint page](https://ryanjosephkamp.github.io/aqrobat/docs/research/prose-qr/phase-05/)
explains the failure through retained traces without presenting a failed grid as
successful phone candidates. [Evidence and custody](https://github.com/ryanjosephkamp/aqrobat/tree/codex/aqrobat-foundation/docs/research/prose-qr/phase-05)
preserve all outcomes and the safe continuation prompt.

## Verification

Phase 05: 40 candidate captures, 120 failed exact-payload attempts; six conventional
controls, 18 passing control attempts. Two algorithm families and three probe
profiles. Default ZXing receives only the QR format restriction. Readers receive
unchanged final pixels, with expected content compared afterward. Source matrices
and locations stay in the renderer/diagnostics, never forced extraction or repair.

All 40 immediate native repeat PNG hashes match. The 384 full-paragraph model
proposals are separate from native captures/payload probes, with reconstructable
word changes, scores and hashes. Model/native RGBA hashes differ for all four
selected cases; a read-only audit checks retained endpoint pixels and provenance.
Executed superseded sources are archived. All 460 prior inventory files and all
three product download hashes remain unchanged. Final logical-file custody stays
below this phase's 12-MB safeguard.

18/18 product unit tests, full formatting, Git whitespace and scoped-file checks
pass. The handback fits a 390-pixel browser viewport, its diagram controls use
retained run counts, its TXT download matches the checkpoint, and a simulated
blocked-clipboard fallback selects the correct prompt. No page errors or automatic
external requests. These are headless browser checks, not phone acceptance.
Product build/site/text-browser results remain earlier checks on unchanged code;
this research does not rebuild or replace product downloads.

## Known issues

No accepted readable, inconspicuous, meaningful prose with ordinary-reader and
normal-phone scanning. Words are fabricated/repetitive; tighter typography may
crowd letters. Uniform 700 stays separate from the regular-weight target. A
perfect target-center score does not establish finder topology or payload recovery.
The canvas optimization model is not pixel-identical to native browser text.
Prior phase-04 small-image replay reproduces only six of eight original hashes.

No new phone, physical-print, Gmail, native clipboard or owner installed-extension
observations. Exact earlier phone-case denominator and conditions remain unknown.
Emoji atlas and dedicated project migration remain deferred. Further captures
need a fresh modest phase with a preregistered native-letter finder objective.
Research is incomplete; this checkpoint is not a completed-prose claim.
