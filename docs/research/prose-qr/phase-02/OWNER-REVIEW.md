# Owner review and direction

October 8, 2026. Ryan reviewed the first pilot by voice transcription, without
exporting the HTML observations. This note preserves that distinction.

- The examples look like QR codes, not ordinary prose or text.
- Internal blank areas and repeated word tiles miss the intended paragraph structure.
- Many letters are too small or tightly packed to identify without changing settings.
- Ryan did not scan these examples. His confidence that they could scan is an
  expectation, **not a phone scan observation**; it does not override the retained
  software failures.
- The next structural target is visible letters, ordinary single word spaces,
  continuous lines, and plausible punctuation. Fabricated words and nonsensical
  content are acceptable. Semantic or grammatical quality is not yet the gate.
- Weight changes and careful letter choices are welcome. Ryan authorized further
  creative, systematic exploration, with no fixed time deadline, preserving the
  computer and other work. Check appearance and legibility before another handback.

Continue in this chat/branch. Keep product exporters and extension unchanged,
PR #1 draft, npm private/unpublished, and all first-pilot evidence intact. No new
chat, agent, host, release, migration, or message is needed for this work.

The next experiment uses text lines, not one isolated word tile per QR module.
It measures structural constraints before scanning: minimum native type size,
single spaces, no empty lines or blank module-sized regions, natural line spacing,
and no glyph overlap. Grayscale ink, if tested, is an explicitly separate styled
variant; it is never described as plain text or a uniform-black-text result.

Resource policy for this continuation: one test-owned headless browser at a time,
sequential CPU work, bounded batches, and an initial 30-MB incremental evidence
budget. Stop expanding a failing grid and inspect its cause. Commit verified
milestones. These are operational safeguards, not a claim that reaching a cap
satisfies the prose goal.
