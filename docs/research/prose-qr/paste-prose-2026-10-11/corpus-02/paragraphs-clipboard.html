small drawing of a leaf. Sam checked the shelf, found two green folders, and paused. One held recipes; the other held
plans for the garden. Instead of guessing, Sam carried both to the porch. The note’s author laughed and opened the
garden plans. They spent the afternoon deciding where to put a bench. The recipes remained on a nearby chair, and
