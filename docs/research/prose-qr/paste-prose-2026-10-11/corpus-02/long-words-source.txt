The committee discussed interoperability, accessibility, reproducibility, and responsibility before choosing a demonstration. These were useful words, but a visitor would need a concrete explanation. The demonstration therefore began with an ordinary paragraph and a visible change. Participants could inspect the source, compare the output, and ask what had been preserved. A misunderstanding was corrected before the next session. Nobody shortened a troublesome word to make the arrangement look better. If a requirement could not be met, the record said so. The explanation became more understandable when every important claim pointed to something another person could examine.
