Warm waves moved around the wooden wharf while Morgan watched a wide-winged gull above the water. Down below, a
workman moved an enormous brown box from a wagon. “Wow,” whispered Mae, who had never seen the old warehouse
open. Windows glowed along the upper wall. Someone was making room for a weekend exhibition of maps, models, and
photographs. Morgan wondered how many journeys had begun beside those doors. Mae wondered whether the enormous
box held a model boat. Both questions seemed worth keeping, even though neither could be answered from the wharf.
