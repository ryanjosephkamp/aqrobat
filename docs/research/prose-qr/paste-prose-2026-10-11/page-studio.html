Latin letters, digits, and punctuation are supported. Unsupported scripts, missing fonts, and material that cannot fit are rejected. Paragraph spacing is normalized in the displayed rows; original TXT keeps the raw source.

A standard page

This is compact type. Fitting checks letter height and whole words; it does not predict whether a phone will scan.

1. Open the Word file

Choose the same paper size as the receiving document. Use Word with Impact available. Keep the exported table at its original width and spacing.

2. Copy, then paste

Select all in Word and copy. Paste into a standard page using Keep Source Formatting. Copying a whole document can also copy its paper settings. The native Word-file route has been tested; the browser Copy button has not been verified end to end.

3. Scan the result

Check the exact link on the actual pasted document. Changing words, type, spacing, or the receiving app can change scanning.

No AI service, remote font, or generation server. No hidden image or solid QR modules. Google Docs and physical printing remain untested in this run.
