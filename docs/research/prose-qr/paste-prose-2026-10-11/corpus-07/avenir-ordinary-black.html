On Saturday we repaired the gate behind the library. The hinge was loose, the paint was old, and one board had split near the bottom. We
brought a brush, a small tin of paint, and the tools we could carry. The work took longer than expected because the rain returned twice.
Between showers, we sat beneath the roof and listened to a reader describe her favorite book. When the gate finally closed without scraping
the path, we stood back to look. It was a modest change, but people could use the entrance comfortably again. On Saturday we repaired
