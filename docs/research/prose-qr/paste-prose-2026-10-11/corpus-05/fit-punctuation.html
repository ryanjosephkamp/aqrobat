The sign said: “Open the box (carefully), then count what’s inside.” There were pencils, postcards, and a note marked
‘Read me first.’ Alex asked, “Is this everyone’s work—or just today’s?” The answer was written on the back: some
pieces were finished; others were waiting for a second pair of eyes. Alex made a list: names, dates, questions.
One postcard had an exclamation mark! Another ended with an ellipsis… Nobody corrected the writers’ punctuation.
