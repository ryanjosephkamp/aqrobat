A folded note waited on the kitchen table. “Please bring the green folder,” it began. There was no signature,
only a small drawing of a leaf. Sam checked the shelf, found two green folders, and paused. One held recipes;
the other held plans for the garden. Instead of guessing, Sam carried both to the porch. The note’s author
laughed and opened the garden plans. They spent the afternoon deciding where to put a bench. The recipes
remained on a nearby chair, and later provided a useful answer to a completely different question. A folded
