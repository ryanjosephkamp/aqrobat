SCENT OF RAIN. A LITTLE BIRD RESTED NEAR THE GARDEN WHILE WE MADE WARM TEA.

WE WOULD TAKE LATER. EVERY SMALL SOUND SEEMED CLEAR IN THE STILL AIR. THE
