## Method and limits

Pitch is the recorded mean of horizontal and vertical five-run scales; each axis scale is its run sum divided by seven. Separate axis scales preserve anisotropy. Primary offsets use the unrounded selected contour centroid; alternate results use the rounded model sample point. inkBounds contains inclusive min/max grayscale<128 sample coordinates, not a counter box, Range advance box, TextMetrics estimate or scanline extent. Signed outward excess is positive outside the nominal boundary: left=cx−3.5p−xmin, top=cy−3.5p−ymin, right=xmax−cx−3.5p, bottom=ymax−cy−3.5p. Half-pixel support adds 0.5 to every excess under integer sample-center convention; gray>=128 antialias fringe remains UNKNOWN. No pixel was recounted. Envelope containment is necessary for the proposed honest placement, never sufficient for native finder or payload acceptance.

Intended first-selected geometry; exactly three selected points; each balance/span/stability >=0.8; nonempty matching scored records with EVERY record having four complete axes and worst RMS <=0.35; four complete true axes with worst RMS <=0.35.

All fourteen PNG hashes match their saved render receipts; aggregate rows equal the per-family receipts. Source aliases are separately hashed. Filled resources have null models: pitch/native eligibility are UNKNOWN, not assumed from outlines. All seven outlines have complete saved four-axis models; six exceed RMS 0.35. Arial Hebrew ם alone passes that standalone metric (0.298261630), but its envelope fails and it cannot replace Latin O/o without changing exact prose. Copperplate O (0.361324723) remains a calibration reference, not a qualified finder. Other families remain failed diagnostic references; none is an accepted carry-forward glyph.

Resources-02 manifest starts stroke 4; retained result and source use Silom 4.5 and Arial Hebrew 4.25 after saved stem adjustment. Results use actual recorded styles. Current common.mjs has the explicit every-record gate; historical manifest script hashes describe historical sources and are not silently treated as current hashes.
