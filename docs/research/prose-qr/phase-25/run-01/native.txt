THIS PAGE STUDIES HOW ORDINARY LETTERS CAN CARRY A SECOND VISUAL STRUCTURE WHILE REMAINING READABLE TEXT.
A READER SHOULD BE ABLE TO FOLLOW EACH SENTENCE AT ITS NORMAL SIZE. A CAMERA SEES THE SAME LETTERS AS A
PATTERN OF LIGHT AND DARK MARKS. THOSE TWO VIEWS NEED TO AGREE BEFORE THE EXPERIMENT CAN BE USEFUL. THE
SHAPE OF A LETTER DEPENDS ON ITS FONT, ITS SIZE, AND THE SPACE AROUND IT. A SMALL CHANGE IN LINE SPACING CAN
AFFECT A SCANNER WITHOUT CHANGING THE WORDS. DIFFERENT READERS ALSO PROCESS AN IMAGE IN DIFFERENT WAYS.
ONE SUCCESSFUL RESULT DOES NOT TELL US HOW EVERY PHONE WILL BEHAVE. WE PRESERVE THE ORIGINAL TEXT AND
RECORD EVERY TEST, INCLUDING FAILURES. THE GOAL IS A PAGE THAT REMAINS CLEAR TO A PERSON AND WORKS WITH AN
ORDINARY READER. A SEPARATE IMAGE EXPORT CAN BE USEFUL, BUT IT DOES NOT PROVE THAT TEXT WILL KEEP ITS LAYOUT IN
ANOTHER APPLICATION. CAREFUL OBSERVATION HELPS US DISTINGUISH A PROMISING RESULT FROM A FINISHED TOOL. THIS
