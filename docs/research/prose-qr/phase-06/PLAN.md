## Frozen first batch: 12 native finder triplets

- Installed Courier, Monaco and Impact, each at native 20 pixels and uniform
  weight 400, black ink on white. Verify actual platform faces before generation.
  Impact is a naturally heavy face; weight 400 does not imply a light appearance.
- Comfortable 24-pixel leading or close leading derived from measured capital
  height plus a 1-pixel allowance. No negative tracking in the first batch.
  Require the global allowed glyph ascent/descent envelope to fit the line height.
- Raw glyph-pixel objective or stronger finder-axis objective. Use a small fixed
  bank of complete English words, upper/lowercase versions, with single spaces
  and punctuation. Semantically coherent prose is not claimed.
- Proportional advances are permitted: the native pre uses the installed font's
  real widths, without per-character boxes. Renderer optimization discretizes
  approximate advances on a quarter-pixel grid; record actual widths/overflow.
  Reject overflow rather than crop or alter characters.
- Each layout has three letter-filled 7×7 finder regions at conventional corner
  positions for a nominal 25-region square, with a five-region outside border.
  The other regions are empty because this is a finder isolation diagnostic,
  not a whole paragraph or QR encoding. Text rows shift upward by half a line to
  avoid placing intended centers on the leading gap. No hidden modules, invisible
  text, outlines, blur, raster scaling/compression or per-word color/weight.

Measure native glyph pixels, advances, DOM baseline and actual font face. Source
finder geometry guides word selection only. The final, unchanged native browser
PNG goes through the pinned unmodified ordinary jsQR binarizer/locator. Assess
whether its returned corner locations/dimension agree with the intended geometry
only afterward. Expected coordinates never force extraction or modify pixels.
Retain the six actual binarized center-axis traces and sustained central black
run lengths. Source-aligned samples remain diagnostics, not payload recovery.

## Controls and gates

A solid finder-only triplet must have correctly located corners under the same
ordinary diagnostic. A separately labeled conventional full QR control encoding
exact `https://example.com/` must pass jsQR, default ZXing and the retained baseline.
Do not interpret payload failure on incomplete finder-only layouts as a failed
link test; they contain no encoded payload and are not phone candidates.

Record exact native PNG/HTML/TXT/layout/source hashes, computed typography,
single-text-node fidelity for each pre, native fit and same-HTML repeat hashes.
Inspect native letter legibility; size alone is not acceptance. A correct locator
is only a diagnostic milestone. No full-QR sweep or phone handoff follows
automatically; preserve this phase's results and select a narrowly bounded
follow-up from the actual finder behavior. Do not claim completion at a time limit.
