# Read-only article-scale feasibility audit

Preregistered before this analysis. Use only the saved context04 native PNG,
capture DOM glyph ledger, and source geometry. No browser, render, recapture,
acceptance reader, locator, or new payload matrix. Zero new native proposals,
resource renders, repeat captures, and reader slots. Cap 1 MB, timeout 30 seconds.

Compute the nominal QR rectangle from the preregistered upper-left finder center,
module pitch and dimension. Count body glyph layout rectangles intersecting that
rectangle and its four-module quiet-zone ring. This is conservative source-layout
accounting, not reader-return geometry or proof that all rectangle pixels contain
ink. Report actual saved native gray128 dark-pixel counts in the nominal symbol
and quiet ring separately. Do not feed sampled or altered pixels into any reader.

Report body advances, ink heights and line-step/pitch ratios from saved DOM
measurements. The enclosing boxes and gray128 sample do not establish independently
controllable QR bits, data capacity, quiet-zone conformance or an impossibility
proof. The report may show an optional source-geometry overlay over an unchanged
embedded image, clearly separated from the native input and disabled by default.
