## What changed our understanding

1. **Scale:** PILL's old counter-only pitch was 3.6667px. Complete five-run H/V
   spans predict 4.0714px and dimension 112.76, near the ordinary reader's
   4.0357px / dimension113. Its original intended125 and true-diagonal failure
   remain unchanged. This diagnoses an old source-model mismatch without
   relabeling old failures.
2. **Topology:** seven glyph hypotheses produced14 small resources. Copperplate
   O improved the full-context true-axis ratios, while its selected quad spans
   remained too short/unbalanced. A Hebrew final mem resource passed source
   ratios but failed full-context dimension/stability; this separate alphabetic
   lane does not establish Latin or logographic success.
3. **Gate calibration:** a conventional QR with circular visible finders
   recovered the exact URL in ZXing and jsQR but failed the strict span/stability
   gates. OpenCV did not decode it. This is one demonstrated false negative of
   the surrogate gate for a modified conventional control. It is not prose,
   not a standards-conformance claim, and not permission to relax native gates.
4. **Copy structure:** early floated initials split words in native selection.
   A one-node `::first-letter` attempt then exposed a browser/preflight failure:
   white initials were invisible because the browser ignored their outline.
   That rejected capture and its3 reader calls remain recorded. A fresh inline
   span treatment asserts actual stroke, keeps Once/Our/Often intact in native
   paragraph selection, and passes the expanded spacing checks. It still fails
   the QR gates, including a true diagonal RMS0.361325 versus0.35.
5. **Data control:** one lexical choice changes many coupled samples, not many
   independent bits. read→scan changed171 source-grid cells and86 centers;
   Small→Minor changed31 cells and16 centers. The corrected article has527
   non-space body glyphs, but only226 glyph boxes intersect the nominal symbol
   and188 are wholly inside. Normal27px leading spans5.73 source modules.
   Source gray128 accounting finds only6.65% dark pixels in that nominal area
   and visible ink in its proposed quiet ring. These are feasibility diagnostics,
   not an encoding-capacity proof, matrix decoder result or impossibility claim.

## Fixed gate and evidence boundaries

The first naturally returned location in each actually executed ordinary branch
must match the source dimension and intended centers. All three selected points
must have quad balance≥0.8, nominal span fraction≥0.8, stable wide-row
fraction≥0.8, complete scored H/V/both diagonal runs with worst normalized
RMS≤0.35, and complete true geometric H/V/both diagonal runs with the same limit.
The resource centroid, source-aligned samples, scored-only passes, and second
returned locations cannot substitute for these measurements. All thresholds
were retained. Passive instrumentation asserted equality with stock returns.

Local agent visual review is separate from owner readability acceptance.
Context04 checks native per-paragraph selection, font identities, computed
outline and conservative glyph bounds. It does not test clipboard portability,
unstyled text, Gmail, a native macOS text app, a phone, or print. The browser's
fonts and CSS remain part of the styled-source contract. The historical phase65
ERRATA and phase72 setup abort remain untouched.

## Why stop before the ceiling

The three-hour limit is a ceiling, not a quota. The new experiments address
source-scale mismatch, different native counter topology, paragraph placement,
body-text coupling and the selection contract. None supplies both a strict
finder and independent control of the data field. Further font/size/offset
variants would repeat the same negative mechanism; revising the gate would
change the authorized acceptance policy. A multiword finder remains an untested
hypothesis, without a measured contour that solves its broken runs and baseline
gaps. I do not have a defensible next native construction under these fixed
rules. The remaining time and render allowance are unspent, not evidence of
success or impossibility.

A useful next step is a read-only review of the circular-control counterexample
and the data-control limitation, followed by an owner decision about a separately
bounded research scope. `CONTINUE.txt` preserves the current rules and asks for
that decision; it does not restart the three-hour allowance or launch work.
