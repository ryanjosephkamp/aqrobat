# Phase52 — native cursive connections, not merely greater ink density

Preregister one small objective: test whether ordinary installed cursive shaping
can produce larger balanced two-dimensional native components while keeping
identifiable words and accepting only very small interline ink collisions.
This differs from phase49's density/line-clearance objective. Native script
connections are part of the visible font, never added strokes or hidden modules.

Exactly32 multirow native word resources plus32 single-line row resources:
two installed local faces SnellRoundhand and SnellRoundhand-Black, native24px,
CSSweight400, normal kerning/ligatures/single spaces/zero tracking, leading24 or30,
eight fixed words mum/mow/hum/mummy (dark pool) and ill/lit/lilt/will (light pool).
One immediate repeat per multirow resource. The regular script and intrinsically
heavy Black face remain separate lanes; neither is ordinary Monaco body prose.
Do not install/download/distribute fonts. Native shaping may make glyph bounds
interleave; that alone is not proof of a pixel collision. Keep bounds and native
pixel evidence distinct.

Use single-line native PNGs solely as renderer diagnostics to measure adjacent-row
ink overlap at the preregistered leading. Reject overlap above1% of single-row
pixels darker than128, source/font substitution, clipped ink, width drift>0.1px,
size below24, negative tracking or native repeat mismatch. No single-row image,
mask, crop or threshold is passed to any acceptance reader. Record actual native
jsQR binarization component widths/heights/balances and occupancy on complete
multirow resources; separate these from any expected source regions. No QR payload
is present. Inspect selected word resources at1:1; merged or unidentifiable words
reject eligibility regardless of numeric metrics. Owner/portable legibility is
untested even if this local inspection passes.

Selection uses leading24 resources only. Require same-font dark/light occupancy
margin>=0.08, both mechanically eligible, and dark actual-binary component with
minimum width/height span>=48px and balance>=0.35. Select maximum minimum span,
then occupancy margin, then fixed font/word order. Preserve all candidates and
rejections. Do not replace a selected pair after a failed visual check. If none
qualifies, the two conditional finder slots remain unattempted, not failures.

At most two conditional finder-only full-context native sources and immediate
repeats, selected face/word pair at24 and30px leading. Native24px/zero tracking,
whole-word-center assignment with actual shaped word/space advances,2240-square
page, pitch64,25-module finder field and five-module text margins. Center2.4modules
as prior sources. One continuous selectable text node, normal spaces. No weighting,
color classes, synthetic bold, blocks, invisible letters or raster resampling.
Only after resource/visual eligibility, preregister the concrete source recipes
before capture. Reject full-native overflow/font/repeat or visibly merged letters.

Use only complete unchanged native PNGs for default OpenCV, default ZXing and
unchanged jsQR, with conventional exact controls and a solid structural control.
Retain actual selected native H/V/both true diagonal runs/quads/balances/stable
rows losslessly. Resource components and source geometry never replace ordinary
returned full-context geometry or selected native structure. No full-QR source,
phone candidate or payload sweep in this phase, even if a diagnostic passes.

Cap4,000,000 logical bytes including source/custody, capture reserve3,400,000.
Fresh metrics-01 and conditional proposal/run directories, preserve every result,
rejection/abort/error/unattempted slot/source and exact denominator. One task-owned
browser/decoder job at a time; check memory. Prior phases/product/downloads/
prototypes/repos/profiles/tasks/jobs preserved; npmprivate, PR1OPENdraft; no agents,
chats/host/migration/merge/release/store/messages. No new phone/print results.
Research remains unsolved; natural semantic prose and normal-page usability remain
separate acceptance requirements.
