# Phase 09: native selected-run structure

October 9, 2026. Baseline `d932bcefeebfd19d85ec3339c3e7fc5086210341`.
Fresh phase; phase 08 is sealed and will not change. Combined source/evidence cap
4,000,000 logical bytes, with capture reserve below 3,000,000. No payloads or
phone candidates are scheduled. One task-owned Chrome instance at a time.

The first batch contains exactly 24 proposed native layouts: six middle words
(`RUM`, `MUM`, `MOM`, `BOB`, `WOW`, `WEB`), two outer-row choices (`wee` / `if`,
or `MUM` / `MUM`), and two installed fonts (Impact and Monaco). All use native
20 px, weight 400, 24 px leading, zero tracking, single ordinary spaces, black
ink, three native text nodes, pitch 6 and source x offset 7. The fixed offset is
the prior phase's source placement, not an image translation. Capture every
proposal that passes source width checks; retain rejections and all failures.
This is a narrow word-cluster test, not a paragraph or a QR encoding.

After the unmodified binarizer and locator return, a passive copy logs their
actual candidate runs and quads. Assert identical locator returns. For each
actual selected point, record horizontal, vertical and both diagonal run arrays,
positive-run completeness, normalized 1:1:3:1:1 error and central-span balance.
Also retain all scored candidates, not only the returned triangle. No expected
coordinate is supplied to the locator or used to choose its pixels.

A structural gate requires an intended ordinary dimension/corner triangle and
all three selected points to have four complete runs, normalized RMS ratio error
at most 0.35 on every axis, quad minimum span at least 80% of nominal central
width, quad minimum/maximum span at least 0.8, and at least 80% stable wide
central rows. This is a conservative research gate, not a decoder standard or
phone claim. Reject overlapping native letter ink bounds, row clearance below
2 px, substituted fonts, clipping or overflow. Mechanical gates do not establish
owner legibility acceptance.

One old seed replay, one conventional payload control tested by three unchanged
reader profiles, one solid finder control, one blank negative, and exact
immediate repeats accompany the batch. Preserve executed source hashes before
each new batch. Any adaptive follow-up needs a new preregistration and exclusive
directory. No full-QR sweep unless complete intended finder structure and clear
letters are demonstrated. Native words remain separate from styled prose and
uniform-black ASCII proofs. No pixel repair, forced corners, acceptance tuning,
hidden blocks, invisible letters or compressed unreadable raster claims.
