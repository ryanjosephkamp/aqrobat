For TextEdit, open RTF or paste formatted text in rich text mode. For websites, keep the Text HTML styles. Email and blog editors may remove styles. TXT cannot store a font or line spacing: preserve every blank and newline, choose the matching monospace font, and test the pasted result. UNDER THE HOOD

Ordinary QR logic.
Unusual ink.

01 / Encode

Nayuki’s MIT-licensed encoder turns your exact input into a standard QR matrix. Error correction and the three familiar corner markers are part of that matrix.

02 / Draw

Aqrobat replaces dark modules with repeated glyphs and leaves light modules as spaces. The canvas uses real font artwork. There is no solid QR hidden behind the characters.

03 / Try

A scanner sees the light and dark pattern. Ink coverage, contrast, font, size, brightness, and camera software all matter. Every new recipe starts untested.

Sizes, emoji, and portability

The old 328, 492, and 656 px choices were presets. Pixel width can vary continuously here. QR matrix dimensions change in four-module steps as payload and error correction require more room; shrinking the artwork does not shrink that matrix.

A custom palette can mix letters, punctuation, and whole emoji sequences, including flags or families. Enter 🤣.☄️1:a or any combination up to 4096 UTF-8 bytes. Unique symbols cycle through dark modules in entry order; duplicate symbols and spaces are ignored. Emoji artwork comes from your system fonts and may change across devices. PNG keeps the rendered artwork; TXT and SVG depend on receiving fonts and spacing. Formatted text and RTF retain layout instructions, but receiving editors can still change typography. Sparse dots and colorful faces are experiments, not reliable defaults.

Short inputs usually need fewer modules. Bigger artwork can help a camera distinguish those modules, but there is no universal minimum scannable size. Decorative text QR is not encryption or a way to hide the payload from machines.

Use with an agent or from the command line

The same deterministic core powers this page, the CLI, and the unpacked extension. An agent chooses settings and calls the tool; it does not need to invent the QR pattern. 

node bin/aqrobat.mjs 'https://example.com' --glyph '#' --density 4x2 --format recipe --out qr.json

The repository includes an npm-ready package layout. It has not been published to npm. Agent instructions · Install the unpacked extension

Prior art and what we are exploring

Text and emoji QR codes already exist. qrcode-terminal offers terminal output and a CLI; node-qrcode provides a general QR package; OshiQR offers text and custom emoji modes. Aqrobat explores a small shared toolchain, reproducible recipes, and visible scan evidence. This is a product direction, not a novelty claim.
