Metrics were measured in the installed Menlo regular/bold and Courier New regular/bold fonts. Chrome’s platform-font report confirmed those fonts for the word probes; font-file hashes are retained. A browser requesting the same font elsewhere does not guarantee the same glyphs.

Dense word tiles can overlap vertically or spill slightly into the intended blank margin. Pixel border measurements and optical line-height ratios are retained per case. The original input labels describe a nominal four-module border, not a guarantee of its exact rendered size.

How the experiment works

The standard encoder supplies a QR matrix. A small original word list supplies high-ink words and lower-ink words. The strict track places complete words with uniform typography. The assisted track can shrink light-region words and change spacing, while keeping actual characters. No hidden black squares or custom font shapes sit behind the words.

Each raw input is a screenshot of the DOM at a recorded size. Its original PNG and pixel hashes are saved. Diagnostic processing uses the whole original image; it does not consult the target matrix to repair pixels. Exact decoded text is compared with the intended payload.

My inference: the results expose a tension between readable word spacing and strong finder/data patterns. The diagnostic passes justify testing optical conditions; they do not establish that an ordinary paragraph can conceal a camera-readable QR.

Custody, limits, and the calibration correction

The first fractional-edge SVG control failed jsQR at 640 px. That failure is retained in pilot-01. Integer pixel boundaries corrected the control, which then passed both decoders at all three sizes.

Large replay HTML files triggered a conservative storage pause. The summary writer also reapplied the reporting reserve; its output was recovered from already flushed receipts. Replay source was archived losslessly, then only unattempted cases and one previously untested scale continued. Existing raw frames were not rerun or removed. Every failure remains recorded.

The full grid finished within the 60-minute execution limit. Stored evidence stayed under 50 MiB, including the lossless replay archives and calibration failure. No model sessions, font downloads, GPU training, email activity, or new hosting surface were created.
